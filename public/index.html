GOOD TO KNOW

Questions,
answered.

A visibility signal isn't a guaranteed explanation for low impressions.

Does X offer an official shadowban status?+

X has visibility controls, search filtering and an “Under the Hood” reporting tool for eligible users. But there is no single public real-time flag that confirms every kind of “shadowban.”

Why are some checks manual?+

Search autocomplete can be tested through a specialist provider, but ghosting and reply ranking can vary by thread, viewer and time. We won't call those automatic without an actual measurement.

Does a missing post mean I'm banned?+

Not necessarily. Search indexing delays, private accounts, sensitive-content filters, ranking, deleted posts or X access issues can also make content disappear. Repeat the observation from an independent account.

Do you store my X password?+

We never request X passwords or authorization. With automatic mode enabled, your entered public username is sent to the site server and its configured data provider; results may be cached. We do not provide a user account system.
